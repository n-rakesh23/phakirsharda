## 1. Project Overview

Create a polished, credible corporate website for **Phakir Sarada
Private Limited**, presenting the company as a multi-sector business
operating across:

-   Mining
-   Construction
-   Manufacturing
-   Transportation and logistics
-   Industrial training

The website should also present the company's community and charitable
work through the **Maa Kalarai Charitable Trust**.

The website should communicate operational capability, reliability,
safety awareness, regional presence, skill development, social
responsibility, and a professional approach to business partnerships.

The site should serve prospective clients, suppliers, contractors,
institutional buyers, job applicants, training candidates, community
partners, donors and volunteers, and other stakeholders.

## 2. Website Goals

1.  Establish a strong and trustworthy digital identity for the company.
2.  Explain the company's five business verticals clearly.
3.  Present its geographic footprint in Odisha and its office presence
    in Bengaluru.
4.  Showcase verified projects, facilities, capabilities, and
    operational milestones.
5.  Generate qualified business enquiries.
6.  Attract applicants for industrial training programmes.
7.  Present the work of the Maa Kalarai Charitable Trust with dignity
    and accuracy, and invite community partnerships.
8.  Make it easy for visitors to contact the correct office or
    department.
9.  Provide a foundation that can grow as the company adds projects,
    services, locations, and business units.

## 3. Brand Positioning

### Desired impression

**Industrial strength, dependable execution, and long-term
partnership.**

The brand should feel established and capable without making unsupported
claims about company size, production capacity, certifications, clients,
or years of experience.

### Brand personality

-   Professional
-   Confident
-   Practical
-   Safety-conscious
-   Modern
-   Regionally rooted, nationally accessible

### Suggested core message

**Building resources. Moving progress. Delivering with purpose.**

This is a proposed website headline direction, not an approved corporate
slogan. Final brand copy should be confirmed by the company.

## 7. Visual Design Direction

### Overall style

A modern industrial-corporate design with strong typography, generous
spacing, high-quality photography, restrained motion, and clear
navigation.

Avoid generic templates, excessive gradients, cluttered dashboards, and
decorative animations that distract from the company's capabilities.

### Official logos

Both logos have been supplied and are stored in `src/assets/brand/`.

**Phakir Sarada Private Limited** (`phakir-sarada-logo.jpeg`, 1280×1280
JPEG on a white background)

-   A circular badge. A teal ring (about `#4B8F9C`) carries "PHAKIR
    SARADA PRIVATE LIMITED" and "INDIA" in white slab-serif capitals.
-   The centre shows a globe with an industrial skyline, a human head
    silhouette with multicoloured gears, and a "P" and swan monogram.
-   It confirms the spelling **Phakir Sarada**.
-   It is very detailed and becomes unreadable below roughly 64 px. The
    header and favicon need a simplified mark, such as the P and swan
    monogram, ideally as an SVG.
-   It has no transparent background, so on dark backgrounds it should
    sit on a white circle or pad.

**Maakalarai Charitable Trust** (`maa-kalarai-trust-logo.webp`, 350×100
WebP, transparent background)

-   A tree formed by two upturned hands in navy (about `#144B71`), with
    red stars as leaves.
-   The wordmark reads "Maakalarai Charitable Trust" in red script
    (about `#AE2029`), with the tagline **"Serving Humanity with
    Compassion"** in navy script.
-   The logo writes the name as one word, "Maakalarai". Use this
    spelling consistently unless the trust says otherwise.
-   The file is low resolution, so it may look soft on high-density
    screens. Request an SVG or a larger PNG.

**Palette reconciliation (decision needed):** the company logo's teal
is not in the proposed navy and gold palette. The options are (a) keep
navy and gold for the site and treat the badge as a self-contained
emblem, (b) add teal as a secondary brand colour, or (c) rebase the
palette on teal. Teal, trust navy, and trust red are defined as tokens
in `src/styles/index.css` so any option can be adopted. The Community
page uses the trust's own navy and red to give it a distinct identity.

### Suggested colour palette

  Role           Colour            Hex
  -------------- ----------------- -----------
  Primary        Deep navy         `#10243A`
  Secondary      Graphite          `#29343D`
  Accent         Industrial gold   `#D6A64F`
  Background     Warm off-white    `#F5F6F4`
  Surface        White             `#FFFFFF`
  Body text      Slate             `#536170`
  Dark section   Near-black navy   `#0A1724`

Use gold as a controlled accent for buttons, highlights, icons, and fine
dividers---not as a large background colour.

### Typography

Use a modern sans-serif family with excellent readability.

Suggested pairing: - Headings: **Manrope** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** -
Body: **Inter**

Use a clear hierarchy: - Large, concise hero heading - Strong section
headings - Short paragraphs - Scannable lists and cards - Comfortable
line length and spacing

### Photography and imagery

Prioritise authentic company photography. Recommended image categories:

-   Mining sites, equipment, and material handling
-   Construction sites and engineering work
-   Manufacturing processes and facilities
-   Trucks, logistics, and material movement
-   Training sessions, workshops, and practical skill demonstrations
-   Charitable trust activities and community programmes
-   Team members and workplace operations
-   Office and location photography

Use licensed stock photography only as temporary placeholders. Never
present stock images as actual company projects or facilities. Replace
placeholders with approved company photographs before launch.

Photographs of trainees, beneficiaries, and community members need
informed consent. Photographs of minors need guardian consent. Show
beneficiaries respectfully and never in a way that exploits hardship.

## 8. Homepage Experience

Recommended homepage sequence:

1.  **Header:** Company logo, navigation, business enquiry CTA.
2.  **Hero:** Strong industrial photograph or video still, concise
    headline, short company introduction, two CTAs.
3.  **Business verticals:** Five clear cards for Mining, Construction,
    Manufacturing, Transportation, and Industrial Training.
4.  **Company overview:** A short introduction and link to About Us.
5.  **Operational footprint:** Office locations and Odisha operating
    districts, clearly differentiated.
6.  **Featured projects:** Approved project highlights, led by the
    Paradip construction activity if the company approves publication.
7.  **Why work with us:** Only substantiated differentiators such as
    documented capabilities, delivery processes, safety practices, or
    regional presence.
8.  **Safety and responsibility:** Publish only policies and initiatives
    that are actually implemented.
9.  **Community -- Maa Kalarai Charitable Trust:** A short, warm
    introduction to the trust, its focus areas, and a link to the
    Community page. Use a visually distinct but on-brand treatment,
    such as the warm off-white background with gold accents.
10. **Enquiry CTA:** Invite project, procurement, vendor, training, and
    partnership enquiries.
11. **Footer:** Navigation, verified contact details, office locations,
    a link to the Maa Kalarai Charitable Trust, legal links, and
    copyright.

## 9. Interaction and Functional Requirements

-   Responsive navigation with a mobile menu.
-   Clear calls to action throughout the site.
-   Enquiry form with fields for name, organisation, email, phone,
    enquiry category, location, and message.
-   Form validation and spam protection.
-   Enquiry delivery to a company-controlled email address or CRM.
-   Click-to-call and click-to-email links on mobile.
-   Map links for verified office addresses.
-   Project and gallery pages that work well on mobile.
-   Accessible keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
-   Useful 404 page and form success/error states.
-   Privacy notice and consent language where required.

-   Training enquiry or application option with only the fields
    needed, such as programme of interest and preferred location.
-   Trust contact and partnership option, routed to the trust's own
    contact address.

Do not collect unnecessary personal information. The final form and
privacy wording should be reviewed before launch.

Do not accept online donations or payments until the trust confirms
its legal status, bank details, receipting process, and any tax
benefits, and until those are reviewed. Until then, use a "Contact the
trust to support" call to action.

## 10. Responsive Design

Design mobile-first and test at these approximate viewport widths:

-   Mobile: 360--430 px
-   Tablet: 768--1024 px
-   Laptop: 1280--1440 px
-   Large desktop: 1600 px and above

Requirements: - No horizontal overflow. - Legible body text and
touch-friendly controls. - Images crop intentionally at each
breakpoint. - Navigation remains usable on small screens. - Tables and
project details adapt to narrow layouts.

## 11. Accessibility and Quality

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA** practices where practical.

-   Sufficient colour contrast.
-   Semantic heading structure.
-   Descriptive alt text for meaningful images.
-   Labels and error messages for form fields.
-   Keyboard-accessible menus and controls.
-   Reduced-motion support.
-   Captions or transcripts for meaningful video content.
-   Avoid text embedded only inside images.

## 12. SEO and Discoverability

Create unique page titles and meta descriptions for each page. Use
descriptive URLs, structured headings, descriptive image filenames, and
meaningful internal links.

Initial keyword themes to validate: - Phakir Sarada Private Limited -
Mining company in Odisha - Mining operations in Keonjhar / Sundargarh /
Jajpur / Mayurbhanj - Construction company in Paradip - Manufacturing
company in Odisha - Transportation and logistics services in Odisha -
Industrial training in Odisha - Maa Kalarai Charitable Trust

These are keyword themes, not claims about rankings or service
eligibility.

Technical SEO checklist: - XML sitemap - `robots.txt` - Canonical URLs -
Open Graph metadata - Favicon and social sharing image -
LocalBusiness/Organization structured data using verified details only -
Search Console and analytics after consent/privacy review - Redirects
and 404 handling

## 13. Performance and Technical Direction

The implementation stack can be finalised in the technical
specification. The design must support:

-   Optimised, responsive images
-   Lazy loading below-the-fold media
-   Minimal JavaScript and third-party scripts
-   Font loading optimisation
-   Caching and compression
-   HTTPS throughout
-   Good Core Web Vitals
-   Secure form handling and server-side validation

Avoid autoplay video with sound. Provide a static image fallback for
large hero media.

## 14. Content and Claims Governance

The website must be accurate and defensible. Before publishing, obtain
company approval for:

-   Legal company name and incorporation details
-   Official logo and brand assets
-   Exact office addresses and contact details
-   Mining locations, mine/lease names, minerals, and operating status
-   Licences, permits, environmental clearances, and other compliance
    statements
-   Construction project scope, client permissions, and progress
-   Manufacturing products, plant details, and capacity
-   Transport fleet size, service coverage, and capacity
-   Industrial training programmes, durations, eligibility, fees (if
    published), accreditation or affiliation, certificates issued, and
    any placement outcomes
-   Maa Kalarai Charitable Trust: registered name, registration
    details, trustees (if published), legal relationship to the
    company, focus areas, initiatives, beneficiary numbers, and any tax
    exemption status (for example 12A/80G)
-   Certifications, memberships, awards, and safety statistics
-   Leadership names, biographies, and photographs
-   Client names, testimonials, and partner logos

Do not invent or imply certifications, government relationships, client
endorsements, project values, production capacity, fleet size, or
operational permissions.

Do not claim training accreditation (for example NSDC, DGT, or a Sector
Skill Council), government-recognised certificates, or job or placement
guarantees unless the company has documented them. Do not claim tax
benefits for donations, beneficiary counts, or impact figures for the
trust unless they are verified.

## 15. Conversion Strategy

The main conversion is a qualified business enquiry.

Use distinct enquiry routes where useful: - Mining enquiry -
Construction enquiry - Manufacturing enquiry - Transport/logistics
enquiry - Industrial training enquiry - Vendor/partner enquiry - Careers
enquiry - Charitable trust / community partnership enquiry

Each route should reach the correct company contact or be clearly
categorised in the enquiry inbox/CRM.
